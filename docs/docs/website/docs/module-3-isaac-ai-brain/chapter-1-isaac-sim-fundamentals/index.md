# Chapter 1: NVIDIA Isaac Sim Fundamentals

This chapter covers the fundamentals of NVIDIA Isaac Sim, which is essential for creating digital twins of humanoid robots and environments to safely simulate physics, sensors, and interactions before real-world deployment.

## Topics Covered

- The role of Isaac Sim in Physical AI
- Photorealistic simulation concepts
- Synthetic data generation for training
- Integrating Isaac Sim with ROS 2

## Learning Objectives

By completing this chapter, you will:

- Understand the fundamental role of Isaac Sim in Physical AI
- Learn to simulate realistic physics using Isaac Sim
- Master the integration between Isaac Sim and ROS 2
- Practice safe testing of humanoid robot movements
- Generate synthetic data for AI model training
